Full-Stack Digital Ordering & Restaurant Management Platform

1. YOUR ROLE

Act as a senior full-stack software engineer, software architect, database engineer, UI/UX designer and restaurant technology consultant.

Design and build a complete, secure, production-oriented digital ordering and restaurant management system for BOURBON PIZZA JUNCTION, Wandegeya, Kampala, Uganda.

The project should be suitable for development and eventual commercial deployment by Slother’s Tech House.

This is not merely a restaurant landing page. It must be a functional full-stack application combining:

9. AUTOMATIC DELIVERY FEES — UGX 1,000 PER KILOMETRE

Implement map-based delivery calculation.

Default delivery origin:

Bourbon Pizza Junction, Wandegeya, Kampala, Uganda.

Default delivery rate:

UGX 1,000 per kilometre.

The rate must be editable through the admin dashboard.

Location selection

Allow customers to:

* Enter a delivery address.
* Select a location on an interactive map.
* Adjust the map pin.
* Enter additional directions for the rider.

Use a suitable geocoding and routing service.

Do not invent coordinates for Bourbon. Have an administrator confirm the exact restaurant location before production deployment.

Route calculation

Calculate the one-way driving distance from the confirmed restaurant location to the customer’s selected delivery location.

Do not use straight-line GPS distance as a substitute for road distance.

Use a genuine routing service, such as a suitably hosted OSRM service or a configured commercial routing API.

The routing provider must be configurable.

Handle:

* Invalid locations.
* Unreachable destinations.
* Routing-service failures.
* Locations outside the delivery area.
* Missing route distances.
* Requests exceeding provider limits.

Do not silently charge a guessed fee if route calculation fails.

Fee formula

Default calculation:

Delivery Fee = ROUND UP (One-Way Route Distance in Kilometres) × UGX 1,000

For example:

* 2.0 km = UGX 2,000.
* 3.4 km = UGX 4,000.
* 5.0 km = UGX 5,000.
* 8.2 km = UGX 9,000.

Use the precise distance returned by the routing provider, convert it to kilometres and apply the configured rounding rule consistently.

The customer must see:

* Delivery destination.
* Calculated route distance.
* Delivery rate per kilometre.
* Rounding rule.
* Final delivery fee.
* Final order total.

Recalculate the fee on the server at checkout.

Do not trust a delivery fee or distance supplied by the browser.

Persist the distance, rate, rounding rule, delivery fee and origin/destination coordinates associated with the order so subsequent configuration changes do not alter historical order totals.

If a route changes or the quote expires, recalculate and request customer confirmation before payment.

Administrator delivery settings

Allow authorised administrators to configure:

* Restaurant location.
* Delivery rate.
* Distance rounding method.
* Maximum delivery distance.
* Delivery enable/disable status.
* Minimum order value.
* Delivery availability hours.
* Manual delivery-fee override, with a required reason and audit log.
* Optional delivery zones and special rules.

For pickup orders, delivery fee must always be zero.

Never calculate a return-trip charge unless the restaurant explicitly changes the policy.

Maps and privacy

Do not expose private routing-service credentials in the browser.

Restrict public map credentials where the provider supports restrictions.

Request location permission only when needed.

Do not store precise customer location beyond what is necessary for fulfilling and recording the order.

Use a clear location privacy notice.
